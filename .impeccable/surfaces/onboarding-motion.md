# Onboarding motion revision

September 10, 2026. One authored native prototype requested by the user. This is a review artifact, not approval to replace production onboarding. Extends the approved blue field-dial composition; no identity tournament or new visual system. Final price remains undecided.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A person understands the relationship between a reading, a reference, and a useful comparison by changing the demonstrated signal themselves.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit MagicCuts Action blue, adaptive canvas, rounded tabular readings, precise measurement ticks and native controls. Daylight equipment setup motivates the light default; dark appearance adapts equally.

STORY: See a signal strengthen; hold a reference and understand the difference; understand the one-time Pro offer; choose a first instrument after purchase. Demonstrations stay clearly labeled.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Native MagicCuts navigation, two-line promise, one open calibrated dial with a dominant reading, a short explanatory sentence and a signal slider. A persistent bottom action advances the lesson. View Pro skips the introduction.

FORM: Local extension of the user-approved field dial, seed 28110c47. Its data geometry carries the motion. The reference remains fixed while the live marker moves; the dial compresses gently into the offer. No sensor or purchase APIs in the standalone prototype.

SIGNATURE: The same visible signal becomes a comparison. Motion is interruptible; Reduce Motion makes value changes immediate and uses short crossfades. Autoplay exists only as a recording option and never drives the ordinary interactive flow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
