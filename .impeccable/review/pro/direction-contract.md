## Direction contract

THESIS: Every reading is a usable instrument: measure, understand variation, establish a reference, compare, record and reuse. The app avoids a gallery of generic metric cards.

OWN-WORLD: Existing adaptive Action blue (#0052C7 light, #1F66D9 dark); matte ink instrument fields; warm-white numerals; cyan signal ink only where contrast requires it. SF rounded measurement numerals and segment labels, native SF body copy, precise tick weights, no glow or faux materials.

STORY: Unlock once, choose a real source, make a deliberate measurement, inspect its time and quality, save a baseline or session, then apply the evidence in a workflow or report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Native Instruments title and source/metric controls; one large live instrument with dominant rounded value; a quieter history chart with shared selection; Record and Set baseline above a native three-section tab bar. Inspection enlarges history; comparison aligns two rulers on the same scale.

FORM: A field chronograph translated into native instrument pages, grounded candidate 6, seed 28110c47; the user's Apple Watch craft reference and approved three compositions govern. The selected source replaces the face's geometry only when its data type requires it.

SIGNATURE: Pin a moment in the chart and all related values follow it; return to live explicitly. Select a baseline and align both recordings. Rounded segment labels, consistent selection haptics, reduced-motion alternatives, and no fake interpolation of measured values.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
