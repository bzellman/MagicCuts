Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Your data and MagicCuts

MagicCuts stores its library on your device by default. We do not operate a server that receives your instrument readings, tag contents, room captures, or usage history. There are no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

What the app stores

Depending on the tools you use and what you save, the local library can include:

  • Bluetooth device names, identifiers, service filters, and proximity settings.
  • Instrument readings, timestamps, source information, baselines, workflows, notes, and reports.
  • NFC tag identifiers and contents, raw NDEF records, capacity, and scan diagnostics.
  • Network request timing and route information, reported cellular technology, service forecasts, and delayed cellular-condition summaries provided by iOS.
  • Room geometry, recognized walls and objects, depth samples, dimensions, an orientation map, a reference camera image, and positions of measurements within a room.

Sound measurements retain numeric levels, not audio recordings. Room positions are relative to the captured room; location-based instruments can separately use location information you permit.

Optional iCloud sync

You can enable iCloud library sync separately on each installation. When enabled, eligible library data, including saved room assets and field captures, is uploaded to your private CloudKit database using your Apple account. Apple provides this service under its own policies. Turning sync off stops further sync on that installation; it does not automatically delete copies already stored in iCloud or on other devices.

Network and nearby-device tools

Network tests send requests to the endpoint you choose. That endpoint and network providers can observe ordinary connection information, including your IP address. Upload tests send generated test bytes. Saved field-test captures omit the endpoint query, but the requested endpoint receives the request itself.

Peer tools communicate with participating devices you connect. The local benchmark uses an ephemeral encrypted connection and asks both users to verify the same connection code before exchanging test traffic or Nearby Interaction tokens. Wi-Fi Aware pairing uses the system pairing interface. These tools do not upload your room library to a peer.

NFC reads begin when you start a scan. A URL found on a tag opens only when you choose to open it; that destination then applies its own policies.

Device permissions and system services

MagicCuts requests access as needed for the selected feature: Bluetooth and local networking for discovery; camera for room/depth capture and orientation; motion for movement instruments; microphone for sound levels; location for location-dependent readings; nearby interaction for participating-device ranging; and Siri for Shortcuts. Availability and permissions are controlled by iOS and can be changed in Settings.

Apple's MetricKit can provide delayed cellular-condition summaries for this app. MagicCuts keeps the supported summary locally as a field capture; it is not sent to a developer analytics service. An enabled iCloud library can sync that saved capture.

Sharing and deletion

Exports and the share sheet send the selected files to the app or destination you choose. Room archives can contain a reference image and orientation map; field-capture JSON can contain raw NFC contents. Review the export's description before sharing. Recipients control copies they receive.

You can delete saved items in the app. A room revision that other saved measurements or unfinished work reference is retained to preserve those measurements' spatial context. Library deletion is propagated through iCloud when sync is enabled and available.

Accounts and purchases

MagicCuts does not require a developer account. Apple handles App Store purchases and optional iCloud access through your Apple account. Saved rooms and records can be viewed offline; network, cloud, and participating-device features require their respective connections.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when the app's data practices change and show the revision date above.
